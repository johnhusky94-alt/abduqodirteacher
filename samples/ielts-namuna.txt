@ielts The Silk Road
The Silk Road was not one road. It was a network of trade routes that connected China with Europe for more than 1,500 years.

Merchants carried silk, spices, paper and glass. They often travelled in large groups called caravans, because the roads were dangerous.

Samarkand was one of the most important cities on the road. Traders from many countries met there to buy and sell goods.
