@quest Unit 4 · Oila (Family and friends)

@circle Kimning?
@rule Egalik so'zlari: kimning?
Bu so'zlar narsa kimga tegishli ekanini bildiradi.

I → my (mening)
you → your (sening, sizning)
he → his (uning: erkak yoki o'g'il bolaning)
she → her (uning: ayol yoki qiz bolaning)
we → our (bizning)
they → their (ularning)

Misol: This is my bag. = Bu mening sumkam.
Diqqat: his = erkakniki, her = ayolniki!

? "my" so'zi nimani bildiradi?
* mening
- sening ! sening = your.
- uning ! uning = his yoki her.
- bizning ! bizning = our.

? "bizning" inglizcha qanday?
- their ! their = ularning.
* our
- your ! your = sening, sizning.
- my ! my = mening.

? Erkak yoki o'g'il bolaga tegishli narsa uchun qaysi so'z ishlatiladi?
* his
- her ! her ayol yoki qiz bola uchun.
- our ! our = bizning.
- my ! my = mening.

? Ayol yoki qiz bolaga tegishli narsa uchun qaysi so'z ishlatiladi?
- his ! his erkak yoki o'g'il bola uchun.
* her
- their ! their = ularning (bir necha kishi).
- your ! your = sening, sizning.

? "ularning" inglizcha qanday?
- they ! they = ular. "ularning" esa their.
* their
- our ! our = bizning.
- his ! his = uning (erkakning).

@gap We are students. ___ teacher is very good. = our ! we → our.
@gap They are doctors. ___ hospital is in the centre of town. = their ! they → their.

@tf This is Jasur. Her school is big. = noto'g'ri ! Jasur o'g'il bola: His school is big.
@tf Zarina and Omina are sisters. Their house is big. = to'g'ri ! Ikki kishi → their.
@tf We are in class 7. Their teacher is very good. = noto'g'ri ! we → our: Our teacher.

@circle 's belgisi

1) She's a nurse. Bu yerda 's = is (u hamshira).
2) Aziz's bag. Bu yerda 's = kimning (Azizning sumkasi).
Ismdan keyin narsa kelsa, bu "kimning" degani.

- Aziz sumka ! 's "kimning" degani: Azizning sumkasi.

- Madina book ! 's tushib qolgan: Madina's book.

- book's Madina ! Avval egasi, keyin narsa: Madina's book.

? "She's a nurse." Bu yerda 's nima?
* is (u hamshira)
- kimning ! 's dan keyin narsa emas, kasb kelyapti: She is a nurse.

? "This is Ali's school." Bu yerda 's nima?
- is ! "Ali is school" ma'nosiz. Bu Alining maktabi.
* kimning (Alining maktabi)

? "He's married." Bu yerda 's nima?
* is (u uylangan)
- kimning ! married = uylangan. He is married.

? Qaysi gap to'g'ri?
* What's Ali's job?
- What's Ali job? ! 's tushib qolgan: Ali's job.
- What's Alis job? ! Apostrof kerak: Ali's.
- What Ali's job? ! What's = What is. is tushib qolgan.

@circle has, likes, works
@rule he, she, it bilan fe'lga -s qo'shiladi
I, you, we, they bilan fe'l o'zgarmaydi:
I like football. We work hard. They have a house.

he, she, it bilan fe'lga -s qo'shiladi:
He likes football. She works in a hospital. It loves the garden.

Diqqat! have → has ("haves" degan so'z yo'q!)
I have a sister. → She has a sister.

like = yoqtirmoq, love = juda yaxshi ko'rmoq, work = ishlamoq, have = ... bor (ega bo'lmoq)

? he, she, it bilan fe'lga nima qo'shiladi?
* -s
- hech narsa qo'shilmaydi ! he, she, it bilan -s kerak: He likes.

? I, you, we, they bilan-chi?
- -s ! I, you, we, they bilan -s qo'shilmaydi: We like.
* hech narsa qo'shilmaydi

? "She ... a brother." Qaysi so'z to'g'ri?
- have ! she → has.
* has
- haves ! "haves" degan so'z yo'q: has.
