Eng yaqin do'stingiz haqida yozing (40–80 so'z):
- ismi va yoshi
- qayerdan va qayerda yashaydi
- oilasi (has ... brothers / sisters)
- nimani yoqtiradi (likes ...)
- u qanday odam: nice, kind, funny
Shu so'zlarni ishlating: is, has, likes, lives, his / her.
